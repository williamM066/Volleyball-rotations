Rotation 1

What you do, in order

    Your zone in each rotation

    Net is at the top. Rotation numbers follow the setter: rotation 1 = setter in zone 1. Win the serve back → everyone moves one zone clockwise.

    Cheat sheet

    Serve receive uses three passers: both outsides + the back-row middle (no libero). Positions are overlap-legal until the serve is contacted.
